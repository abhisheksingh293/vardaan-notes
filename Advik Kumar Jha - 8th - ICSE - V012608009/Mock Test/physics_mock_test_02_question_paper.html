Physics Mock Test 02
Class: 8 ICSE Subject: Physics Time: 1 Hour Max. Marks: 40
General Instructions:
  • All questions are compulsory.
  • The paper is divided into three sections: A, B, and C.
  • Write your answers neatly and legibly. Draw diagrams wherever necessary.
SECTION A (10 Marks)(Objective Type - Attempt all questions)
Q1. The SI unit of moment of force is: [2]
(a) Newton
(b) Newton-metre
(c) Joule
(d) Pascal
Q2. When a ray of light passes obliquely from an optically denser medium to a rarer medium, it: [2]
(a) bends towards the normal
(b) bends away from the normal
(c) goes undeviated
(d) reflects back completely
Q3. The anomalous expansion of water dictates that the density of water is maximum at: [2]
(a) 0°C
(b) 100°C
(c) 4°C
(d) -4°C
Q4. A substance has a mass of 50 g and a volume of 20 cm³. If placed in water, it will: [2]
(a) float on water
(b) sink in water
(c) suspend exactly in middle
(d) dissolve instantly
Q5. According to the Kinetic Theory, which state of matter has the highest kinetic energy among its particles? [2]
(a) Solid
(b) Liquid
(c) Gas
(d) Equal in all states
SECTION B (15 Marks)(Short Answer Type - 3 Marks Each)
Q6.
Define atmospheric pressure. Why do deep-sea divers have to wear specially designed heavy-duty suits? [3]
Q7.
State three fundamental differences between a real image and a virtual image. [3]
Q8.
A force of 50 N is applied at a perpendicular distance of 0.2 m from the pivot of a door. Calculate the moment of force. What happens to the turning effect if the distance from the pivot is doubled while keeping the force same? [3]
Q9.
Explain the phenomenon of dispersion of white light. Name the colors of the visible spectrum obtained in the correct order. [3]
Q10.
Give scientific reasons for the following:
(a) A partially inflated balloon expands when placed in hot water.
(b) Liquids exert pressure on the walls of the container holding them. [1.5+1.5]
SECTION C (15 Marks)(Long Answer Type - 5 Marks Each)
Q11.
(a) Describe an experiment using a Eureka can to measure the volume and density of an irregular solid (e.g., a stone) that sinks in water.
(b) A solid of mass 120 g displaces 40 cm³ of water. Find its density in SI units (kg/m³). [3+2]
Q12.
(a) Draw a neat, labelled ray diagram showing the refraction of light through a rectangular glass slab. Clearly mark the incident ray, refracted ray, emergent ray, angle of incidence (\(i\)), and angle of emergence (\(e\)).
(b) Define lateral displacement and state one factor on which its magnitude depends. [3+2]
Q13.
(a) Differentiate between Density and Relative Density.
(b) Explain the principle of floatation. Using this principle, explain why a massive ship made of iron and steel floats on water, whereas a small solid iron nail sinks. [2+3]
