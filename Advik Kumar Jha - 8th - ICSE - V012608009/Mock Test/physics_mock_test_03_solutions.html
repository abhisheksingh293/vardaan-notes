DETAILED SOLUTIONS: Physics Mock Test 03 (Class 8 ICSE)
Question 1: Multiple Choice Questions
[5 Marks]
(i) If the mass of a moving body is halved and its velocity is doubled, its kinetic energy becomes:
Answer: (b) Double
Explanation: Initial \( KE = \frac{1}{2}mv^2 \). New mass \( m' = \frac{m}{2} \), new velocity \( v' = 2v \).
New \( KE = \frac{1}{2}(\frac{m}{2})(2v)^2 = \frac{1}{2}(\frac{m}{2})(4v^2) = 2 \times (\frac{1}{2}mv^2) \). So, it becomes double.
(ii) The commercial unit of electrical energy is:
Answer: (c) Kilowatt-hour
Explanation: While Joule is the SI unit, electrical energy consumed in households and industries is commercially measured in Kilowatt-hour (kWh), also simply known as 'units'.
(iii) The distance between the pole and the principal focus of a spherical mirror is called:
Answer: (b) Focal length
Explanation: By definition, focal length (\( f \)) is the linear distance between the pole (P) of the mirror and its principal focus (F).
(iv) A mirror highly preferred as a rear-view mirror in vehicles is:
Answer: (c) Convex mirror
Explanation: Convex mirrors are used as rear-view mirrors because they always form erect, virtual, and diminished images, providing a much wider field of view of the traffic behind.
(v) The rate of evaporation of a liquid decreases with:
Answer: (b) Increase in humidity
Explanation: Humidity is the amount of water vapour present in the air. If the air is already highly humid, its capacity to hold more water vapour is low, thereby decreasing the rate of evaporation.

Question 5: Solve the following numericals
[6 Marks]

(i) Machine power calculation: [2 Marks]

Given: Force (Load) \( F = 500 \text{ N} \), Displacement (height) \( d = 15 \text{ m} \), Time \( t = 5 \text{ s} \).

\[ \text{Work Done (W)} = F \times d = 500 \text{ N} \times 15 \text{ m} = 7500 \text{ J} \] \[ \text{Power (P)} = \frac{\text{Work Done}}{\text{Time}} = \frac{7500 \text{ J}}{5 \text{ s}} = 1500 \text{ W} \]

(ii) Work done by the boy: [2 Marks]

Given: Mass \( m = 40 \text{ kg} \), Number of steps = 20, Height of one step = 15 cm = 0.15 m.

Total vertical height (\( h \)) = \( 20 \times 0.15 \text{ m} = 3 \text{ m} \).

\[ \text{Work done against gravity (W)} = mgh \] \[ W = 40 \text{ kg} \times 10 \text{ m/s}^2 \times 3 \text{ m} = 1200 \text{ J} \]

(iii) Kinetic energy of the car: [2 Marks]

Given: Mass \( m = 1000 \text{ kg} \), Velocity \( v = 20 \text{ m/s} \).

\[ \text{Kinetic Energy (KE)} = \frac{1}{2}mv^2 \] \[ KE = \frac{1}{2} \times 1000 \times (20)^2 = 500 \times 400 = 200,000 \text{ J} \text{ (or 200 kJ)} \]

Question 7: Reasoning and Definitions
[8 Marks]

(i) Law of conservation of energy: [2 Marks]

Statement: Energy can neither be created nor destroyed; it can only be transformed from one form to another. The total energy of an isolated system remains constant.

Example: In a glowing electric bulb, electrical energy is transformed into light energy and heat energy.

(ii) Cooling sensation during evaporation of sweat: [2 Marks]

Reason: Evaporation requires latent heat of vaporization. When sweat evaporates from our skin, it absorbs this required heat from our body. Since our body loses heat, its temperature drops, causing us to feel a cooling sensation.

(iii) Principal focus of a convex mirror: [2 Marks]

Definition: It is a point on the principal axis of a convex mirror from which a beam of light rays, initially parallel to the principal axis, appears to diverge after reflection from the mirror.

(iv) Porter doing work against gravity? [2 Marks]

Answer: No, he is doing zero work against gravity.

Reason: The force of gravity (weight of the load) acts vertically downwards, while the displacement of the porter is horizontal along the platform. Since the angle between force and displacement is 90° (\( \cos 90^\circ = 0 \)), the work done against gravity is zero.
