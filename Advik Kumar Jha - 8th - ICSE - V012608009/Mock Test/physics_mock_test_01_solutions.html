DETAILED SOLUTIONS: Physics Mock Test 01 (Class 8 ICSE)
Q1. (i) If the mass of an object is doubled while its velocity remains constant, its kinetic energy becomes:
[1 Mark]
Answer: (b) double
Explanation: Kinetic Energy formula is \(K.E = \frac{1}{2}mv^2\). Since mass (\(m\)) is directly proportional to Kinetic Energy, if mass is doubled, the kinetic energy will also double.
Q1. (ii) A boy A does 500 J of work in 20 seconds, and a boy B does the same amount of work in 10 seconds. The power spent is:
[1 Mark]
Answer: (c) less by boy A than by boy B
Explanation: Power = Work / Time. Boy A takes more time (20s) than Boy B (10s) to do the same work. Since power is inversely proportional to time, Boy A spends less power than Boy B.
Q1. (iii) A ray of light passing parallel to the principal axis of a concave mirror, after reflection:
[1 Mark]
Answer: (d) passes through the principal focus
Explanation: According to the rules for ray diagrams of spherical mirrors, any incident ray parallel to the principal axis converges at the principal focus after reflecting from a concave mirror.
Q1. (iv) Evaporation of a liquid happens:
[1 Mark]
Answer: (b) at all temperatures below its boiling point
Explanation: Evaporation is a slow surface phenomenon that occurs at all temperatures, whereas boiling occurs rapidly only at a specific temperature called the boiling point.
Q1. (v) Assertion (A) & Reason (R) regarding concave mirror used by doctors:
[1 Mark]
Answer: (a) Both A and R are true and R is the correct explanation of A.
Explanation: ENT doctors use a concave mirror worn on their forehead to focus light coming from a lamp onto a small area (like the ear or throat) of the patient, providing a bright, concentrated spot of light.

Q5 (i). An object of mass 50 kg is lifted to a height of 10 m above the ground. Calculate its potential energy. If the object falls, what is its kinetic energy just before hitting the ground?
[3 Marks]

Given: Mass (\(m\)) = 50 kg, Height (\(h\)) = 10 m, Acceleration due to gravity (\(g\)) = 10 m/s\(^2\).

Step 1: Calculate Potential Energy (P.E)

\[ P.E = m \times g \times h \] \[ P.E = 50 \times 10 \times 10 = 5000 \text{ Joules} \]

Step 2: Kinetic Energy before hitting ground

According to the Law of Conservation of Energy, the total mechanical energy remains constant. As the object falls, its potential energy is converted entirely into kinetic energy just before hitting the ground.

Therefore, Kinetic Energy = Initial Potential Energy = 5000 Joules.

Q5 (ii). A machine raises a load of 750 N through a height of 16 m in 5 seconds. Calculate the power at which the machine works.
[3 Marks]

Given: Force/Load (\(F\)) = 750 N, Displacement/Height (\(S\)) = 16 m, Time (\(t\)) = 5 s.

Step 1: Calculate Work Done (\(W\))

\[ \text{Work Done } (W) = F \times S \] \[ W = 750 \text{ N} \times 16 \text{ m} = 12000 \text{ Joules} \]

Step 2: Calculate Power (\(P\))

\[ \text{Power } (P) = \frac{\text{Work Done}}{\text{Time}} \] \[ P = \frac{12000 \text{ J}}{5 \text{ s}} = 2400 \text{ Watts} \]

The power of the machine is 2400 W (or 2.4 kW).

Q6 (ii). Why does a convex mirror always form a virtual, erect, and diminished image? Draw a ray diagram.
[3 Marks]

Reason: A convex mirror is a diverging mirror. When parallel rays of light fall on it, they diverge after reflection. These diverging rays never actually meet in front of the mirror but appear to diverge from a point behind the mirror (the virtual focus). Because the rays only appear to intersect, a virtual, erect, and diminished image is formed regardless of the object's position.

(Students must draw a convex mirror diagram. An object is placed anywhere in front. One ray parallel to the principal axis diverges, appearing to come from F behind the mirror. Another ray directed towards C reflects back along its own path. The extended virtual rays intersect behind the mirror, forming a small, erect image between P and F.)

Q7 (i). State two differences between boiling and evaporation.
[2 Marks]
Boiling Evaporation
Takes place only at a specific temperature (Boiling Point). Takes place at all temperatures below the boiling point.
It is a bulk phenomenon (happens throughout the liquid). It is a surface phenomenon (happens only at the liquid surface).
Q7 (ii). Energy transformations in a stone thrown upwards.
[2 Marks]

Going Up: When the stone is thrown, it has maximum Kinetic Energy (due to its velocity) and zero Potential Energy. As it moves upwards, its velocity decreases while its height increases. Therefore, Kinetic Energy transforms into Potential Energy.

Falling Down: At the highest point, it momentarily stops (max P.E, zero K.E). As it falls back down, its height decreases and velocity increases. Therefore, Potential Energy transforms back into Kinetic Energy.

Q7 (iii). Define the principal focus of a concave mirror.
[2 Marks]

The principal focus of a concave mirror is a point on its principal axis where a beam of light rays, initially parallel to the principal axis, actually meet (converge) after reflection from the mirror.

Q7 (iv). In what two ways can we increase the kinetic energy of a moving body?
[2 Marks]

Since the formula for Kinetic Energy is \(K.E = \frac{1}{2}mv^2\), it can be increased by:

  1. Increasing the mass of the moving body.
  2. Increasing the velocity (speed) of the moving body. (Note: Increasing velocity has a much greater effect since K.E is proportional to the square of the velocity).
