SECTION A (8 Marks)
(i) The value of $(105 \times 95)$ evaluated using algebraic identity is:
[1 Mark]
Correct Option: (a) 9975
Step-by-step Solution:
We can express $105$ as $(100 + 5)$ and $95$ as $(100 - 5)$.
Using the algebraic identity $(a + b)(a - b) = a^2 - b^2$:
$$105 \times 95 = (100 + 5)(100 - 5)$$
$$= 100^2 - 5^2$$
$$= 10000 - 25 = 9975$$
(ii) What percent of $120$ is $30$?
[1 Mark]
Correct Option: (c) 25%
Step-by-step Solution:
Let $x\%$ of $120 = 30$.
$$\dfrac{x}{100} \times 120 = 30$$
$$x = \dfrac{30 \times 100}{120} = \dfrac{300}{12} = 25\%$$
(iii) A length measured as $5.0\text{ cm}$ is incorrectly recorded as $5.25\text{ cm}$. The percentage error is:
[1 Mark]
Correct Option: (b) 5%
Step-by-step Solution:
$$\text{Error} = \text{Recorded value} - \text{True value} = 5.25\text{ cm} - 5.0\text{ cm} = 0.25\text{ cm}$$
$$\text{Percentage Error} = \left( \dfrac{\text{Error}}{\text{True Value}} \right) \times 100\%$$
$$\text{Percentage Error} = \left( \dfrac{0.25}{5.0} \right) \times 100\% = 0.05 \times 100\% = 5\%$$
(iv) Discount in commercial transactions is always calculated on the:
[1 Mark]
Correct Option: (c) Marked Price
Concept Explanation:
Discount is the concession granted by a seller to the buyer on the printed list price, which is known as the Marked Price (MP). Hence, Discount = Marked Price − Selling Price.
(v) For the linear equation $15 - 2(x - 3) = x + 3$, the value of $x$ is:
[1 Mark]
Correct Option: (a) 6
Step-by-step Solution:
Given equation: $15 - 2(x - 3) = x + 3$
$$15 - 2x + 6 = x + 3$$
$$21 - 2x = x + 3$$
Transposing variable terms to LHS and constants to RHS:
$$-2x - x = 3 - 21$$
$$-3x = -18 \implies x = \dfrac{-18}{-3} = 6$$
(vi) The expansion of $(2a - 3b)^2$ is:
[1 Mark]
Correct Option: (b) $4a^2 - 12ab + 9b^2$
Step-by-step Solution:
Using standard algebraic identity $(x - y)^2 = x^2 - 2xy + y^2$:
Here $x = 2a$ and $y = 3b$.
$$(2a - 3b)^2 = (2a)^2 - 2(2a)(3b) + (3b)^2$$
$$= 4a^2 - 12ab + 9b^2$$
(vii) In any rhombus, the diagonals intersect each other at an angle of:
[1 Mark]
Correct Option: (c) $90^\circ$
Geometric Property:
A fundamental property of a rhombus states that its diagonals bisect each other at right angles ($90^\circ$).
(viii) Assertion & Reason on Parallelogram and Rectangle:
[1 Mark]
Correct Option: (b) Both A and R are correct but R is NOT the correct explanation of A.
Explanation:
Assertion (A): If one angle of a parallelogram is $90^\circ$, adjacent angles are supplementary ($180^\circ - 90^\circ = 90^\circ$), so all four angles become $90^\circ$, forming a rectangle. Thus, A is True.
Reason (R): Opposite sides of a parallelogram are equal and parallel. This is a general property of parallelograms and is True.
However, R is not the reason why a parallelogram becomes a rectangle (which depends on angle properties). Hence, R is NOT the correct explanation of A.
SECTION B (8 Marks)
1. In a rhombus $ABCD$, if $\angle A = 72^\circ$, find the measures of $\angle B$ and $\angle C$.
[2 Marks]
Solution:
In rhombus $ABCD$:
1. Opposite angles are equal:
$$\angle C = \angle A = 72^\circ$$
2. Adjacent angles are supplementary ($\angle A + \angle B = 180^\circ$):
$$72^\circ + \angle B = 180^\circ$$
$$\angle B = 180^\circ - 72^\circ = 108^\circ$$
Final Answer: $\angle B = 108^\circ$ and $\angle C = 72^\circ$.
2. Solve for $x$: $\dfrac{x - 5}{3} = \dfrac{x - 9}{5}$
[2 Marks]
Solution:
Cross-multiplying both sides:
$$5(x - 5) = 3(x - 9)$$
$$5x - 25 = 3x - 27$$
Rearranging terms:
$$5x - 3x = -27 + 25$$
$$2x = -2$$
$$x = \dfrac{-2}{2} = -1$$
Final Answer: $x = -1$.
3. Find the value using standard algebraic identity: $(0.8)^2 - (0.2)^2$.
[2 Marks]
Solution:
Using the identity $a^2 - b^2 = (a + b)(a - b)$:
Here, $a = 0.8$ and $b = 0.2$.
$$(0.8)^2 - (0.2)^2 = (0.8 + 0.2)(0.8 - 0.2)$$
$$= (1.0) \times (0.6) = 0.6$$
Final Answer: $0.6$.
4. Evaluate: $15\%$ of $240 - 20\%$ of $95 + 12\%$ of $400$.
[2 Marks]
Solution:
Evaluating each term individually:
$$15\% \text{ of } 240 = \dfrac{15}{100} \times 240 = 36$$
$$20\% \text{ of } 95 = \dfrac{20}{100} \times 95 = 19$$
$$12\% \text{ of } 400 = \dfrac{12}{100} \times 400 = 48$$
Now substituting values back into the expression:
$$\text{Expression} = 36 - 19 + 48 = 17 + 48 = 65$$
Final Answer: $65$.
SECTION C (12 Marks)
1. A number is first increased by $20\%$ and then this increased number is decreased by $20\%$. Find the net percentage change in the number.
[3 Marks]
Solution:
Let the original number be $100$.
Step 1: Increase the number by $20\%$:
$$\text{Increased value} = 100 + \left(\dfrac{20}{100} \times 100\right) = 120$$
Step 2: Decrease the new number ($120$) by $20\%$:
$$\text{Decrease amount} = \dfrac{20}{100} \times 120 = 24$$
$$\text{Final value} = 120 - 24 = 96$$
Step 3: Net percentage change:
$$\text{Net Decrease} = 100 - 96 = 4$$
$$\text{Net Percentage Decrease} = \left(\dfrac{4}{100}\right) \times 100\% = 4\%$$
Final Answer: Net percentage change is a $4\%$ decrease.
2. A man sells a watch for ₹$1,320$ and gains $10\%$. At what price should he sell another watch of the same kind to gain $15\%$?
[3 Marks]
Solution:
Case 1: Find the Cost Price ($\text{CP}$) of the watch.
Given: $\text{Selling Price (SP}_1\text{)} = \text{₹}1,320$, $\text{Gain} = 10\%$
$$\text{CP} = \dfrac{\text{SP} \times 100}{100 + \text{Gain}\%} = \dfrac{1320 \times 100}{100 + 10} = \dfrac{1320 \times 100}{110} = 12 \times 100 = \text{₹}1,200$$
Case 2: Find the required Selling Price ($\text{SP}_2$) to gain $15\%$.
Given: $\text{CP} = \text{₹}1,200$, Desired $\text{Gain} = 15\%$
$$\text{SP}_2 = \text{CP} \times \left(\dfrac{100 + \text{Gain}\%}{100}\right) = 1200 \times \dfrac{115}{100} = 12 \times 115 = \text{₹}1,380$$
Final Answer: He should sell the watch for ₹$1,380$.
3. Factorise completely:
[6 Marks]
a) $x^3 - x^2 - xy + x + y - 1$ [2 Marks]
Grouping terms effectively:
$$= (x^3 - x^2) - (xy - y) + (x - 1)$$
$$= x^2(x - 1) - y(x - 1) + 1(x - 1)$$
Taking $(x - 1)$ common:
$$= (x - 1)(x^2 - y + 1)$$
b) $a^2 + 6ab + 9b^2 - 16c^2$ [2 Marks]
Grouping first three terms as a perfect square:
$$= \left(a^2 + 2(a)(3b) + (3b)^2\right) - (4c)^2$$
$$= (a + 3b)^2 - (4c)^2$$
Applying $x^2 - y^2 = (x + y)(x - y)$:
$$= (a + 3b + 4c)(a + 3b - 4c)$$
c) $(6y^2 - 54) \div (y - 3)$ [2 Marks]
Factorising the numerator:
$$6y^2 - 54 = 6(y^2 - 9) = 6(y^2 - 3^2) = 6(y + 3)(y - 3)$$
Dividing by $(y - 3)$:
$$\dfrac{6(y + 3)(y - 3)}{y - 3} = 6(y + 3) = 6y + 18$$
SECTION D (12 Marks)
1. A mother is $28$ years older than her daughter. In $4$ years, the mother's age will be three times the age of her daughter. Find their present ages.
[4 Marks]
Solution:
Let the present age of the daughter be $x$ years.
Then, the present age of the mother $= (x + 28)$ years.
Ages after $4$ years:
$$\text{Daughter's age} = (x + 4) \text{ years}$$
$$\text{Mother's age} = (x + 28) + 4 = (x + 32) \text{ years}$$
According to the given condition:
$$\text{Mother's age} = 3 \times (\text{Daughter's age})$$
$$x + 32 = 3(x + 4)$$
$$x + 32 = 3x + 12$$
$$32 - 12 = 3x - x$$
$$20 = 2x \implies x = 10$$
Present Ages:
$$\text{Daughter's present age} = x = 10 \text{ years}$$
$$\text{Mother's present age} = 10 + 28 = 38 \text{ years}$$
Final Answer: Daughter is $10$ years old and Mother is $38$ years old.
2. $PQRS$ is a parallelogram whose diagonals intersect at point $O$. If $\angle POS = 60^\circ$, $\angle QSR = 28^\circ$, and $\angle SQR = 32^\circ$, find: (i) $\angle RPS$, (ii) $\angle PRS$.
[4 Marks]
Solution:
Given: $PQRS$ is a parallelogram with diagonals intersecting at $O$.
$\angle POS = 60^\circ$, $\angle QSR = 28^\circ$, $\angle SQR = 32^\circ$.
(i) To find $\angle RPS$:
Since $PQ \parallel SR$ and $QS$ is a transversal, alternate interior angles are equal:
$$\angle PSQ = \angle SQR = 32^\circ$$
In $\triangle POS$, the sum of interior angles is $180^\circ$:
$$\angle OPS + \angle POS + \angle PSO = 180^\circ$$
$$\angle RPS + 60^\circ + 32^\circ = 180^\circ$$
$$\angle RPS + 92^\circ = 180^\circ \implies \angle RPS = 180^\circ - 92^\circ = 88^\circ$$
(ii) To find $\angle PRS$:
$\angle SOR$ and $\angle POS$ form a linear pair along diagonal $PR$:
$$\angle SOR = 180^\circ - \angle POS = 180^\circ - 60^\circ = 120^\circ$$
In $\triangle OSR$, applying angle sum property:
$$\angle SRO + \angle SOR + \angle OSR = 180^\circ$$
$$\angle PRS + 120^\circ + 28^\circ = 180^\circ$$
$$\angle PRS + 148^\circ = 180^\circ \implies \angle PRS = 180^\circ - 148^\circ = 32^\circ$$
Final Answer: (i) $\angle RPS = 88^\circ$, (ii) $\angle PRS = 32^\circ$.
3. A shopkeeper marks his goods at $25\%$ above the cost price and then allows a discount of $12\%$ on the marked price. Find his overall gain or loss percentage.
[4 Marks]
Solution:
Let the Cost Price ($\text{CP}$) = ₹$100$.
Step 1: Calculate Marked Price ($\text{MP}$):
$$\text{MP} = 100 + 25\% \text{ of } 100 = 100 + 25 = \text{₹}125$$
Step 2: Calculate Discount ($12\%$ on $\text{MP}$):
$$\text{Discount Amount} = \dfrac{12}{100} \times 125 = \dfrac{3}{25} \times 125 = 3 \times 5 = \text{₹}15$$
Step 3: Calculate Selling Price ($\text{SP}$):
$$\text{SP} = \text{MP} - \text{Discount} = 125 - 15 = \text{₹}110$$
Step 4: Determine overall Gain/Loss:
Since $\text{SP} (\text{₹}110) > \text{CP} (\text{₹}100)$, there is a gain.
$$\text{Gain Amount} = \text{SP} - \text{CP} = 110 - 100 = \text{₹}10$$
$$\text{Gain Percentage} = \left(\dfrac{\text{Gain}}{\text{CP}}\right) \times 100\% = \left(\dfrac{10}{100}\right) \times 100\% = 10\%$$
Final Answer: Overall Gain = $10\%$.
