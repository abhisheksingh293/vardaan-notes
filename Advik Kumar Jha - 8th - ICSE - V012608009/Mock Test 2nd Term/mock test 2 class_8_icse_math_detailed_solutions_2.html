SECTION A — SOLUTIONS (8 Marks)
(i) Value of $(104 \times 96)$ using algebraic identity.
[1 Mark]
Correct Option: (a) 9984
We can express $104 \times 96$ as $(100 + 4)(100 - 4)$.
Using identity $(a + b)(a - b) = a^2 - b^2$:
$$\begin{aligned} (100 + 4)(100 - 4) &= 100^2 - 4^2 \\ &= 10000 - 16 \\ &= 9984 \end{aligned}$$
(ii) What percent of $150$ is $45$?
[1 Mark]
Correct Option: (c) 30%
Required Percentage $= \left( \dfrac{\text{Given Value}}{\text{Total Value}} \times 100 \right)\%$
$$= \left( \dfrac{45}{150} \times 100 \right)\% = \left( \dfrac{3}{10} \times 100 \right)\% = 30\%$$
(iii) Measured length $= 5.25\text{ cm}$, Actual length $= 5.0\text{ cm}$. Percentage Error?
[1 Mark]
Correct Option: (b) 2.5%
Error $= |8.2\text{ cm} - 8.0\text{ cm}| = 0.2\text{ cm}$
Percentage Error $= \left( \dfrac{\text{Error}}{\text{Actual Value}} \times 100 \right)\%$
$$= \left( \dfrac{0.2}{8.0} \times 100 \right)\% = \dfrac{20}{8}\% = 2.5\%$$
(iv) Profit or Loss percentage is always calculated on:
[1 Mark]
Correct Option: (c) Cost Price
By commercial rules, Profit or Loss percentage is always evaluated taking Cost Price (C.P.) as the base.
(v) Solve $12 - 3(x - 2) = 2x + 3$.
[1 Mark]
Correct Option: (a) 3
$$12 - 3x + 6 = 2x + 3$$
$$18 - 3x = 2x + 3$$
$$18 - 3 = 2x + 3x \implies 5x = 15 \implies x = 3$$
(vi) Expansion of $(3a - 2b)^2$.
[1 Mark]
Correct Option: (b) $9a^2 - 12ab + 4b^2$
Using identity $(x - y)^2 = x^2 - 2xy + y^2$:
$$(3a - 2b)^2 = (3a)^2 - 2(3a)(2b) + (2b)^2 = 9a^2 - 12ab + 4b^2$$
(vii) Adjacent angles in a parallelogram are:
[1 Mark]
Correct Option: (b) Supplementary
Consecutive (adjacent) interior angles between two parallel lines sum up to $180^\circ$ (supplementary).
(viii) Assertion (A) & Reason (R) on Square and Rhombus.
[1 Mark]
Correct Option: (a) Both A and R are correct and R is the correct explanation of A.
A square satisfies all properties of a rhombus (all sides equal and perpendicular diagonals), making it a special rhombus with right angles.
SECTION B — SOLUTIONS (8 Marks)
1. Parallelogram $ABCD$ with $\angle A = (2x + 10)^\circ$ and $\angle B = (3x - 10)^\circ$. Find $x$ and all angles.
[2 Marks]
Step 1: Adjacent angles of a parallelogram are supplementary.
$$\angle A + \angle B = 180^\circ$$
$$(2x + 10) + (3x - 10) = 180 \implies 5x = 180 \implies x = 36^\circ$$
Step 2: Calculate the angle measures:
$$\angle A = 2(36) + 10 = 82^\circ$$
$$\angle B = 3(36) - 10 = 98^\circ$$
Since opposite angles are equal: $\angle C = \angle A = 82^\circ$ and $\angle D = \angle B = 98^\circ$.
Ans: $x = 36^\circ$; $\angle A = 82^\circ, \angle B = 98^\circ, \angle C = 82^\circ, \angle D = 98^\circ$.
2. Solve for $x$: $\dfrac{2x - 3}{4} = \dfrac{x + 1}{3}$
[2 Marks]
Cross-multiplying both sides:
$$3(2x - 3) = 4(x + 1)$$
$$6x - 9 = 4x + 4$$
$$6x - 4x = 4 + 9 \implies 2x = 13 \implies x = \dfrac{13}{2} = 6.5$$
Ans: $x = \dfrac{13}{2} \text{ or } 6.5$.
3. Evaluate using identity: $(1.2)^2 - (0.8)^2$.
[2 Marks]
Using identity $a^2 - b^2 = (a - b)(a + b)$ where $a = 1.2$ and $b = 0.8$:
$$(1.2)^2 - (0.8)^2 = (1.2 - 0.8)(1.2 + 0.8)$$
$$= (0.4) \times (2.0) = 0.8$$
Ans: $0.8$.
4. Evaluate: $20\%$ of $350 + 15\%$ of $180 - 25\%$ of $120$.
[2 Marks]
$$\text{Term 1} = \dfrac{20}{100} \times 350 = 70$$
$$\text{Term 2} = \dfrac{15}{100} \times 180 = 27$$
$$\text{Term 3} = \dfrac{25}{100} \times 120 = 30$$
$$\text{Final Value} = 70 + 27 - 30 = 67$$
Ans: $67$.
SECTION C — SOLUTIONS (12 Marks)
1. Net percentage change in salary when increased by $10\%$ and then decreased by $10\%$.
[3 Marks]
Let the original salary $= \text{₹}100$.
Increase of 10%: New salary $= 100 + \left(\dfrac{10}{100} \times 100\right) = \text{₹}110$.
Decrease of 10%: Final salary $= 110 - \left(\dfrac{10}{100} \times 110\right) = 110 - 11 = \text{₹}99$.
Net decrease $= 100 - 99 = \text{₹}1$.
$$\text{Net Percentage Change} = \left(\dfrac{1}{100} \times 100\right)\% = 1\% \text{ decrease.}$$
Ans: Net salary decreases by $1\%$.
2. S.P. $= \text{₹}2,850$ gives $14\%$ gain. Find S.P. to gain $8\%$.
[3 Marks]
Step 1: Find Cost Price (C.P.)
$$\text{C.P.} = \dfrac{\text{S.P.} \times 100}{100 + \text{Gain}\%} = \dfrac{2850 \times 100}{114} = \text{₹}2,500$$
Step 2: Find new S.P. for $8\%$ gain
$$\text{New S.P.} = \text{C.P.} \times \left(\dfrac{100 + \text{New Gain}\%}{100}\right)$$
$$\text{New S.P.} = 2500 \times \dfrac{108}{100} = 25 \times 108 = \text{₹}2,700$$
Ans: ₹$2,700$.
3. Factorise completely:
[6 Marks]
a) $x^3 - 3x^2 - x + 3$ [2 Marks]
Grouping in pairs:
$$= x^2(x - 3) - 1(x - 3) = (x - 3)(x^2 - 1)$$
$$= (x - 3)(x - 1)(x + 1)$$
b) $4a^2 - 12ab + 9b^2 - 25c^2$ [2 Marks]
Recognising perfect square in first 3 terms:
$$= (2a - 3b)^2 - (5c)^2$$
Using $X^2 - Y^2 = (X - Y)(X + Y)$:
$$= (2a - 3b - 5c)(2a - 3b + 5c)$$
c) $(8y^2 - 32) \div (2y + 4)$ [2 Marks]
$$= \dfrac{8(y^2 - 4)}{2(y + 2)} = \dfrac{8(y - 2)(y + 2)}{2(y + 2)}$$
Cancelling $(y + 2)$ and simplifying $\dfrac{8}{2} = 4$:
$$= 4(y - 2) \text{ or } 4y - 8$$
SECTION D — SOLUTIONS (12 Marks)
1. Age Problem: Father is 3 times as old as son. In 12 years, father will be twice son's age.
[4 Marks]
Let present age of son $= x$ years.
Present age of father $= 3x$ years.
After 12 years:
Son's age $= x + 12$
Father's age $= 3x + 12$
According to question:
$$3x + 12 = 2(x + 12)$$
$$3x + 12 = 2x + 24 \implies 3x - 2x = 24 - 12 \implies x = 12$$
Present age of son $= 12$ years.
Present age of father $= 3 \times 12 = 36$ years.
Ans: Son's age $= 12$ years, Father's age $= 36$ years.
2. Rhombus $ABCD$ with $\angle OAB = 35^\circ$. Find $\angle ABO, \angle ADC, \angle BCD$.
[4 Marks]
(i) Find $\angle ABO$:
Diagonals of a rhombus intersect at $90^\circ$, so $\angle AOB = 90^\circ$.
In $\triangle AOB$: $\angle ABO = 180^\circ - (90^\circ + 35^\circ) = 55^\circ$.
(ii) Find $\angle ADC$:
Diagonals bisect vertex angles, so $\angle ABC = 2 \times \angle ABO = 2 \times 55^\circ = 110^\circ$.
Opposite angles are equal: $\angle ADC = \angle ABC = 110^\circ$.
(iii) Find $\angle BCD$:
Adjacent angles are supplementary: $\angle BCD = 180^\circ - 110^\circ = 70^\circ$.
Ans: (i) $55^\circ$, (ii) $110^\circ$, (iii) $70^\circ$.
3. Goods marked $30\%$ above C.P. and $15\%$ discount allowed. Overall gain/loss percentage?
[4 Marks]
Let Cost Price (C.P.) $= \text{₹}100$.
Marked Price (M.P.) $= 100 + 30\% \text{ of } 100 = \text{₹}130$.
$$\text{Discount} = 15\% \text{ of M.P.} = \dfrac{15}{100} \times 130 = \text{₹}19.50$$
$$\text{Selling Price (S.P.)} = 130 - 19.50 = \text{₹}110.50$$
$$\text{Profit} = \text{S.P.} - \text{C.P.} = 110.50 - 100 = \text{₹}10.50$$
$$\text{Gain Percentage} = \left(\dfrac{10.50}{100} \times 100\right)\% = 10.5\% \text{ or } 10\frac{1}{2}\%$$
Ans: Overall Gain $= 10.5\%$.
