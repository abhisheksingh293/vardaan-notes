Chapter 4: Energy (Numericals)
Student Name: ____________________________________ Class: 8 (ICSE) Subject: Physics
Section A: Calculation of Work Done
1.
A force of 300 N moves a body through a distance of 4 m in the direction of the force. Calculate the work done by the force using the formula $W = F \times d$.
2.
A machine moves a load of 750 N by a distance of 8.5 m vertically up. Calculate the work done by the machine.
3.
A coolie raises a box of mass 60 kg to a vertical height of 4 m. Calculate the work done by the coolie if the force of gravity on 1 kg mass is 10 N.
4.
Calculate the work done when a force of 120 N moves an object by 15 m in the direction of the force.
5.
A boy pushes a heavy boulder with a force of 500 N, but the boulder does not move. Calculate the work done by the boy.
Section B: Gravitational Potential Energy
6.
What is the potential energy of a stone of mass 15 kg that is lifted to a height of 12 m? Take $g = 10 \text{ N kg}^{-1}$.
7.
A body of mass 8 kg is taken from a height of 4 m to 10 m. Find the increase in the potential energy of the body using the relation $\Delta U = mg(h_2 - h_1)$. Take $g = 10 \text{ N kg}^{-1}$.
8.
A vessel containing 80 kg of water is placed at a height of 20 m above the ground. What is the potential energy stored in the water? Take $g = 10 \text{ N kg}^{-1}$.
9.
A body of mass 5.5 kg falls from a height of 6.0 m. How much energy does it possess at its highest point? Take $g = 10 \text{ N kg}^{-1}$.
10.
Calculate the mass of a body if its potential energy is 2500 J when raised to a height of 25 m. Take $g = 10 \text{ N kg}^{-1}$.
Section C: Kinetic Energy
11.
The mass of a body is 25 kg. It is moving with a speed of 12 m s-1. What is its kinetic energy? Use the relation $K.E. = \frac{1}{2} m v^2$.
12.
A bullet of mass 50 g has its kinetic energy equal to 400 J. Find the speed of the bullet.
13.
Find the increase in kinetic energy of a body of mass 600 g when its speed increases from 4 m s-1 to 8 m s-1.
14.
Two bodies have masses in the ratio 1:2 and are moving with the same speed. Compare their kinetic energies.
15.
An object of mass 12 kg has a kinetic energy of 600 J. Calculate its velocity.
Section D: Power and Energy Transfer
16.
A boy has to do 450 J of work in 1.5 minutes to lift luggage to the roof of a bus. How much power does he spend? Use $P = \frac{W}{t}$.
17.
An electric heater of power 4 kW is used for 2 minutes. Find the energy supplied by the heater.
18.
A machine performs 3600 J of work in 60 s. What is the power of the machine?
19.
How much time will a 150 W motor take to perform 4500 J of work?
20.
A pump is used to lift 150 kg of water from a well 50 m deep, in 25 s. If the force of gravity on 1 kg is 10 N, find the work done by the pump.
21.
From the previous question, calculate the potential energy stored in the lifted water.
22.
Calculate the power spent by the pump in question 20.
23.
If the pump operates at 100% efficiency, state its power rating in kW.
24.
A car engine does 150,000 J of work in 10 seconds. Calculate its power output.
25.
A crane lifts a load of 2000 N to a height of 15 m in 30 seconds. Calculate the power of the crane.
