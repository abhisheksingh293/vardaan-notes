Question 1(5)
Choose the correct answers to the questions from the given options.

(i) If the mass of an object is doubled while its velocity remains constant, its kinetic energy becomes:
(a) half
(b) double
(c) four times
(d) no change
(ii) A boy A does 500 J of work in 20 seconds, and a boy B does the same amount of work in 10 seconds. The power spent is:
(a) same by both boys
(b) more by boy A than by boy B
(c) less by boy A than by boy B
(d) nothing can be said
(iii) A ray of light passing parallel to the principal axis of a concave mirror, after reflection:
(a) passes through the centre of curvature
(b) passes through the pole
(c) becomes parallel to the principal axis
(d) passes through the principal focus
(iv) Evaporation of a liquid happens:
(a) at its boiling point only
(b) at all temperatures below its boiling point
(c) only above its boiling point
(d) when pressure is increased
(v) Assertion (A): A concave mirror can be used as a doctor's head mirror.
Reason (R): A concave mirror reflects light rays and converges them to a focus to illuminate the examined area.
(a) Both A and R are true and R is the correct explanation of A.
(b) Both A and R are true and R is not the correct explanation of A.
(c) Assertion is true but reason is false.
(d) Assertion is false but reason is true.
