Force and Friction - Numerical Problem Bank
Student Name: ____________________________________ Class: 11th Subject: Physics
Section A: Newton's Laws & Basic Force
1.
A constant force acting on a body of mass $3.0 \text{ kg}$ changes its speed from $2.0 \text{ m/s}$ to $3.5 \text{ m/s}$ in $25 \text{ s}$. The direction of the motion of the body remains unchanged. What is the magnitude and direction of the force?
2.
A bullet of mass $0.04 \text{ kg}$ moving with a speed of $90 \text{ m/s}$ enters a heavy wooden block and is stopped after a distance of $60 \text{ cm}$. Calculate the average resistive force exerted by the block on the bullet.
3.
A machine gun fires a bullet of mass $40 \text{ g}$ with a velocity of $1200 \text{ m/s}$. The man holding it can exert a maximum force of $144 \text{ N}$ on the gun. How many bullets can he fire per second at the most?
4.
A car of mass $1000 \text{ kg}$ is moving with a velocity of $10 \text{ m/s}$. If the velocity-time graph for this car is a horizontal line parallel to the time axis, what is the net force acting on the car? Explain your answer.
5.
A passenger of mass $72 \text{ kg}$ is standing in an elevator. Find the apparent weight of the passenger when the elevator is (a) accelerating upwards at $2 \text{ m/s}^2$, and (b) accelerating downwards at $2 \text{ m/s}^2$. (Take $g = 10 \text{ m/s}^2$)
6.
Two masses of $8 \text{ kg}$ and $12 \text{ kg}$ are connected at the two ends of a light inextensible string that goes over a frictionless pulley. Find the acceleration of the masses and the tension in the string when the masses are released. (Take $g = 10 \text{ m/s}^2$)
Section B: Impulse & Conservation of Momentum
7.
A batsman deflects a ball by an angle of $45^\circ$ without changing its initial speed which is equal to $54 \text{ km/h}$. What is the impulse imparted to the ball? (Mass of the ball is $0.15 \text{ kg}$).
8.
A rocket with a lift-off mass $20,000 \text{ kg}$ is blasted upwards with an initial acceleration of $5.0 \text{ m/s}^2$. Calculate the initial thrust (force) of the blast. (Take $g = 9.8 \text{ m/s}^2$).
9.
A shell of mass $0.020 \text{ kg}$ is fired by a gun of mass $100 \text{ kg}$. If the muzzle speed of the shell is $80 \text{ m/s}$, what is the recoil speed of the gun?
10.
Two identical billiard balls strike a rigid wall with the same speed but at different angles, and get reflected without any change in speed. Ball A strikes perpendicularly, while Ball B strikes at an angle of $30^\circ$ with the wall. Calculate the ratio of the magnitudes of impulses imparted to the balls by the wall.
11.
A force acting on a particle of mass $2 \text{ kg}$ varies with time $t$ as $F = (3t^2 - 2t) \text{ N}$. Find the impulse delivered to the particle between $t = 0$ to $t = 2 \text{ s}$, and the corresponding change in momentum.
Section C: Static and Kinetic Friction
12.
A block of mass $10 \text{ kg}$ is placed on a rough horizontal surface. The coefficient of static friction is $\mu_s = 0.5$ and kinetic friction is $\mu_k = 0.4$. Find the frictional force when a horizontal force of (a) $30 \text{ N}$, and (b) $60 \text{ N}$ is applied on the block. (Take $g = 10 \text{ m/s}^2$).
13.
A heavy box of mass $20 \text{ kg}$ is pulled on a horizontal surface by applying a horizontal force. If the coefficient of kinetic friction between the box and the surface is $0.25$, calculate the force required to keep the box moving with a constant velocity.
14.
An automobile vehicle has a mass of $1500 \text{ kg}$. What must be the force between the vehicle and road if the vehicle is to be stopped with a negative acceleration of $1.7 \text{ m/s}^2$? Does this force depend on the coefficient of friction?
15.
A mass of $4 \text{ kg}$ rests on a horizontal plane. The plane is gradually inclined until at an angle $\theta = 15^\circ$ with the horizontal, the mass just begins to slide. What is the coefficient of static friction between the block and the surface?
16.
A block of mass $5 \text{ kg}$ is sliding on a rough horizontal surface with an initial velocity of $10 \text{ m/s}$. If the coefficient of kinetic friction is $0.2$, how much distance will it cover before coming to rest?
17.
A worker pushes a $50 \text{ kg}$ crate horizontally with a force of $250 \text{ N}$. The coefficient of kinetic friction is $0.3$. Calculate the acceleration of the crate. (Take $g = 9.8 \text{ m/s}^2$).
Section D: Motion on an Inclined Plane
18.
A block is released from rest at the top of a smooth inclined plane of inclination $30^\circ$. Find its acceleration down the plane. If the length of the incline is $5 \text{ m}$, find the time taken to reach the bottom.
19.
A block of mass $2 \text{ kg}$ is placed on a rough inclined plane making an angle of $30^\circ$ with the horizontal. The coefficient of static friction is $0.7$ and kinetic friction is $0.6$. Calculate the frictional force acting on the block. Will the block slide?
20.
Calculate the minimum force required to push a block of mass $10 \text{ kg}$ up a rough inclined plane of angle $45^\circ$ with constant velocity. The coefficient of kinetic friction between the block and the plane is $0.2$. (Take $g = 10 \text{ m/s}^2$).
21.
A body of mass $m$ slides down a rough inclined plane of inclination $\theta$ with constant velocity. Prove that the coefficient of kinetic friction $\mu_k = \tan \theta$.
22.
An object takes twice as long to slide down a $45^\circ$ rough inclined plane as it takes to slide down a perfectly smooth $45^\circ$ incline of the same length. Calculate the coefficient of kinetic friction between the object and the rough incline.
Section E: Connected Bodies & Complex Friction
23.
Two blocks of masses $m_1 = 4 \text{ kg}$ and $m_2 = 2 \text{ kg}$ are in contact with each other on a frictionless horizontal surface. A horizontal force of $30 \text{ N}$ is applied to $m_1$. Find the contact force between the two blocks.
24.
Repeat the previous problem (Q23) assuming the horizontal surface is rough, and the coefficient of kinetic friction for both blocks is $0.2$. Does the contact force change? Calculate the new contact force and the acceleration of the system.
25.
A block A of mass $2 \text{ kg}$ rests on a block B of mass $8 \text{ kg}$ which rests on a horizontal table. The coefficient of friction between A and B is $0.2$ and between B and the table is $0.1$. A horizontal force of $25 \text{ N}$ is applied to block B. Will block A slip over block B? Find the acceleration of both blocks.
26.
A mass of $10 \text{ kg}$ is placed on an inclined plane of angle $30^\circ$. It is connected by a string passing over a frictionless pulley at the top of the incline to a hanging mass of $8 \text{ kg}$. If the coefficient of kinetic friction on the incline is $0.1$, find the acceleration of the system and the tension in the string.
27.
A small coin is placed on a flat horizontal turntable. The turntable is observed to make $3$ revolutions in $3.14 \text{ s}$. What is the coefficient of static friction between the coin and the turntable if the coin is observed to slide off when it is more than $10 \text{ cm}$ from the center?
28.
A uniform chain of length $L$ and mass $M$ is lying on a smooth table and one-third of its length is hanging vertically down over the edge of the table. If $g$ is acceleration due to gravity, calculate the work required to pull the hanging part on to the table.
