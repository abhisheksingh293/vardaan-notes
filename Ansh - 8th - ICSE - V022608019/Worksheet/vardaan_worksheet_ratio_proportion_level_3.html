Mathematics Practice Worksheet: Ratio & Proportion (Level 3)
Student Name: ____________________________________ Class: 8 Subject: Mathematics (ICSE)
Section A: Advanced Algebraic Ratios
1.
If $a:b = \frac{1}{3}:\frac{1}{4}$ and $b:c = \frac{1}{2}:\frac{1}{5}$, find the continuous ratio $a:b:c$ in its simplest form.
2.
Two numbers are in the ratio $5:7$. If $9$ is subtracted from each of them, their new ratio becomes $7:11$. Find the difference between the original numbers.
3.
What quantity must be added to each term of the ratio $7:11$ to make it exactly equal to $3:4$?
4.
If the ratio $(3x - 5y) : (2x + y) = 1 : 4$, calculate the numerical value of $x : y$.
5.
Divide an amount of ₹ 3450 among three persons A, B, and C such that A receives $\frac{2}{3}$ of what B receives, and B receives $\frac{1}{4}$ of what C receives. Determine C's share.
6.
If $(4x^2 - 3y^2) : (2x^2 + 5y^2) = 12 : 19$, determine the ratio of $x : y$. (Assume $x$ and $y$ are positive integers).
7.
Derive a generalized formula for the quantity that must be subtracted from each term of the ratio $x:y$ so that it becomes equal to $c:d$.
Section B: Proportion & Continued Proportion
8.
Calculate the exact fourth proportional to the decimal values $0.2$, $0.12$, and $0.3$.
9.
Find the algebraic mean proportional between the expressions $a^3b$ and $ab^3$.
10.
Find the third proportional to the algebraic expressions $(a^2 - b^2)$ and $(a + b)$.
11.
If the numbers $8$, $x$, and $50$ are in continued proportion, determine the positive numerical value of $x$.
12.
What specific number must be logically added to each of the distinct numbers $5, 11, 19,$ and $37$ so that the resulting sums are in proportion?
13.
What number must be subtracted from each of the numbers $17, 25, 31,$ and $47$ so that the remainders form a perfect proportion?
14.
If $\frac{a}{b} = \frac{c}{d} = \frac{e}{f} = \frac{1}{3}$, evaluate the expression $\frac{2a + 3c - 4e}{2b + 3d - 4f}$ using the properties of equal ratios.
Section C: Commercial Application & Word Problems
15.
The monthly incomes of A and B are in the ratio $3:2$ and their monthly expenditures are in the ratio $5:3$. If each person successfully saves ₹ 1000 per month, determine A's monthly income.
16.
A bag contains ₹ 1, 50p, and 25p coins stacked in the ratio $5:6:8$ respectively. If the total monetary value in the bag is ₹ 420, calculate the exact number of 50p coins.
17.
The present ages of two individuals, A and B, are in the ratio $4:5$. Exactly eight years from now, the ratio of their ages will transition to $5:6$. Find their present ages.
18.
A certain sum of money is divided meticulously among P, Q, and R such that for every ₹ 1 that P receives, Q receives 75p and R receives 50p. If P gets ₹ 250 more than R, calculate the total sum divided.
19.
In a co-educational school, the ratio of boys to girls is initially $4:3$. If 20 more girls enroll in the school, the ratio perfectly balances to $1:1$. Find the initial number of boys.
20.
The ratio of the original prices of two bicycles was $16:23$. Two years later, the price of the first bicycle increased by $10\%$ and the second by ₹ 477. The new ratio of their prices became $11:20$. Find the original price of the first bicycle.
21.
The operational expenses of a hotel are partly fixed and partly varying directly with the number of guests. For 20 guests, the total expense is ₹ 3000. For 40 guests, it is ₹ 5000. Find the total expense for hosting 50 guests.
Section D: Mixtures and Alloys
22.
Two identical casks contain liquid mixtures of water and wine in the ratios $2:3$ and $3:4$ respectively. If equal volumetric quantities are extracted from both and mixed into a new container, find the ratio of water to wine in this new mixture.
23.
A large tank contains 80 liters of a milk and water solution comprising exactly 10% water. How many liters of pure water must be added to this solution to increase the water concentration to 20%?
24.
Zinc and copper are melted down together to form an alloy in the ratio $9:11$. What is the total combined weight of the melted mixture if exactly 28.8 kg of pure zinc has been consumed in its creation?
25.
Two distinct vessels contain mixtures of milk and water in the ratios $7:2$ and $5:1$ respectively. In what strict proportion should the two fluid mixtures be combined to obtain a final composite containing milk and water in the ratio $9:2$?
Section E: Variation & Advanced Unitary Method
26.
A military garrison consisting of 1200 men is heavily provisioned for 60 days. After 15 days, 300 men are redeployed and leave the garrison. How long will the remaining food provisions last for the reduced personnel?
27.
A team of 45 men can manually dig a canal 15 km long in a span of 20 days. How many capable men are required to dig a similar 9 km long canal in just 15 days?
28.
An express train travels a distance of 280 km uniformly in 4 hours. How much time (in hours) will it take to smoothly cover a distance of 420 km maintaining the exact same speed?
29.
12 professional typists working intensely for 8 hours a day can complete typing a full manuscript in 15 days. How many days will it take for a team of 10 typists working 9 hours a day to complete the identical task?
30.
If 5 exceptionally quick spiders can catch 5 flies in precisely 5 minutes, how many flies can 100 identical spiders theoretically catch in 100 minutes?
31.
A master contractor undertook to complete a massive infrastructure project in 150 days and immediately employed 80 men. After 90 days of work, he inspected and found only $\frac{1}{4}$ of the work was done. How many extra men must he employ immediately to guarantee the work finishes exactly on the original deadline?
32.
A physical quantity $P$ varies directly as $Q$ and inversely as the square of another quantity $R$. If $P = 10$ when $Q = 5$ and $R = 2$, find the numerical value of $P$ when $Q = 10$ and $R = 4$.
Section F: Properties Challenge Questions
33.
The four interior angles of an irregular quadrilateral are strictly in the ratio $2:3:5:8$. Find the precise geometric measure of the largest interior angle in degrees.
34.
Given that $a:b = 3:4$ and $b:c = 8:9$, calculate the exact numerical value of the fractional expression $\frac{a+b}{b+c}$.
35.
If $x = \frac{2ab}{a+b}$, deeply evaluate the expression $\frac{x+a}{x-a} + \frac{x+b}{x-b}$ without substituting random numbers. (Assume $a \neq b$).
