Section A: Multi-Step Direct Variation & Rates

3.
The cost of $3 \text{ dozen}$ oranges is ₹ $180$. Find the cost of $25$ oranges.
4.
A train covers $420 \text{ km}$ in $6 \text{ hours}$. How much distance will it cover in $4.5 \text{ hours}$ at the same uniform speed?
5.

9.

10.

11.

12.

13.

Section B: Best Buy Comparisons & Inverse Variation
14.

18.

25.
