Mathematics Mock Test 01 (DAV Pattern)
Class: 8 CBSE Subject: Mathematics Time: 2 Hours Max. Marks: 80
General Instructions:
  • The question paper consists of 38 questions divided into 5 sections: A, B, C, D, and E.
  • Section A: Q.1 to Q.20 are MCQs carrying 1 mark each.
  • Section B: Q.21 to Q.25 are Very Short Answer type questions carrying 2 marks each.
  • Section C: Q.26 to Q.31 are Short Answer type questions carrying 3 marks each.
  • Section D: Q.32 to Q.35 are Long Answer type questions carrying 5 marks each.
  • Section E: Q.36 to Q.38 are Case-Based questions carrying 4 marks each.
  • All questions are compulsory. Use of calculators is not permitted.

SECTION E (12 Marks)(Case-Based Questions - 4 Marks Each)
36.
Case Study 1: Electronics Sale
Rohan went to a mall to buy a laptop for his online classes. The marked price of the laptop was ₹ 50,000. During a festive sale, the shopkeeper offered successive discounts of 10% and 5% respectively. After the discounts, a GST of 18% is applied on the discounted price.
(i) Find the price of the laptop after the first discount of 10%. (1 Mark)
(ii) Find the final selling price of the laptop before GST is applied. (1 Mark)
(iii) Calculate the amount of GST to be paid. (1 Mark)
(iv) Find the total bill amount paid by Rohan. (1 Mark)
37.
Case Study 2: School Garden
A school decides to develop a rectangular herbal garden in their backyard. The length of the garden is given by the algebraic expression \( (3x + 4) \) meters and the breadth is given by \( (2x - 3) \) meters. The boundary of the garden needs to be fenced.
(i) Find the expression for the total perimeter of the garden. (1 Mark)
(ii) Find the algebraic expression for the total area of the garden. (2 Marks)
(iii) If the value of \( x \) is 5 meters, find the numerical value of the perimeter. (1 Mark)
38.
Case Study 3: School Picnic
Class 8 of DAV Public School is going on a picnic. The total number of students in the class is 60. The class teacher noticed a pattern in the attendance: the number of boys going to the picnic is 6 more than twice the number of girls.
(i) If the number of girls is taken as '\( y \)', formulate a linear equation to represent the total number of students. (1 Mark)
(ii) Solve the equation to find the total number of girls going to the picnic. (1 Mark)
(iii) Find the number of boys going to the picnic. (1 Mark)
(iv) If each student pays ₹ 250 for the picnic, what is the total collection from the boys? (1 Mark)
