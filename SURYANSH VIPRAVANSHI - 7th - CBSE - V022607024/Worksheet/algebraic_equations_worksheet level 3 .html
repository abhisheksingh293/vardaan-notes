Section B: Applied Word Problems (Numbers & Ages)
11.
The sum of three consecutive multiples of $8$ is $888$. Find the three multiples.
12.
Two numbers are in the ratio $5:3$. If they differ by $18$, what are the two numbers?
13.
A rational number is such that when you multiply it by $\frac{5}{2}$ and add $\frac{2}{3}$ to the product, you get $-\frac{7}{12}$. What is the number?
14.
A father is $30$ years older than his son. In $12$ years, the man will be three times as old as his son. Find their present ages.
15.
The sum of the ages of a father and his son is $50$ years. Five years ago, the father's age was $7$ times the son's age. Find their present ages.
16.
In a two-digit number, the unit's digit is $3$ more than the ten's digit. If the sum of the digits is $11$, find the original number.
17.
The numerator of a fraction is $4$ less than the denominator. If $1$ is added to both its numerator and denominator, it becomes $\frac{1}{2}$. Find the fraction.
18.
Divide $184$ into two parts such that one-third of one part may exceed one-seventh of the other part by $8$.
19.
A number is $56$ greater than the average of its third, quarter, and one-twelfth. Find the number.
20.
Ten years ago, Person A was half of Person B in age. If the ratio of their present ages is $3:4$, what is the sum total of their present ages?
Section C: Money, Distance, & Geometry
21.
I have a total of ₹ $300$ in coins of denomination ₹ $1$, ₹ $2$, and ₹ $5$. The number of ₹ $2$ coins is $3$ times the number of ₹ $5$ coins. The total number of coins is $160$. How many coins of each denomination are with me?
22.
A purse has only ₹ $2$ and ₹ $5$ coins. The total value of the coins is ₹ $136$. If the number of ₹ $2$ coins is one-third the number of ₹ $5$ coins, find the number of each type of coin.
23.
The perimeter of a rectangular swimming pool is $154 \text{ m}$. Its length is $2 \text{ m}$ more than twice its breadth. What are the length and the breadth of the pool?
24.
The base of an isosceles triangle is $\frac{4}{3} \text{ cm}$. The perimeter of the triangle is $4 \frac{2}{15} \text{ cm}$. What is the length of either of the remaining equal sides?
25.
A steamer goes downstream and covers the distance between two ports in $4 \text{ hours}$ while it covers the same distance upstream in $5 \text{ hours}$. If the speed of the stream is $2 \text{ km/h}$, find the speed of the steamer in still water.
26.
The distance between two stations A and B is $340 \text{ km}$. Two trains start simultaneously from these stations on parallel tracks to cross each other. The speed of one of them is greater than that of the other by $5 \text{ km/h}$. If the distance between the two trains after $2 \text{ hours}$ of their start is $30 \text{ km}$, find the speed of each train.
27.
A wire of length $86 \text{ cm}$ is bent in the form of a rectangle such that its length is $7 \text{ cm}$ more than its breadth. Find the length and the breadth of the rectangle so formed.
28.
The angles of a triangle are in the ratio $2:3:4$. Form an algebraic equation and find the measure of each angle.
29.
In a class of $42$ students, the number of boys is $\frac{2}{5}$ of the girls. Find the number of boys and girls in the class.
30.
A man leaves half his property to his wife, one-third to his son and the remaining ₹ $60000$ to his daughter. Find the total value of his property.
Section D: Higher Order Thinking Skills (HOTS)
31.
A total of ₹ $80000$ is to be distributed among $200$ persons as prizes. A prize is either of ₹ $500$ or ₹ $100$. Find the number of each prize awarded.
32.
Half of a herd of deer are grazing in the field and three-fourths of the remaining are playing nearby. The rest $9$ are drinking water from the pond. Find the total number of deer in the herd.
33.
An examination consists of $160$ questions. $4$ marks are given for every correct option, and $1$ mark is deducted for every incorrect option. If a student attempts all questions and scores $340$ marks, how many questions did he answer correctly?
34.
There are some benches in a classroom. If $4$ students sit on each bench, then $3$ benches are left unoccupied. If however, $3$ students sit on each bench, then $3$ students are left standing. How many students are there in the class?
35.
Solve for $p$: $\frac{p + 1}{2} - \frac{p - 2}{3} = \frac{p + 4}{5} + \frac{7}{15}$
36.
The digits of a two-digit number differ by $3$. If the digits are interchanged, and the resulting number is added to the original number, we get $143$. What can be the original number?
37.
The difference between two positive integers is $36$. The quotient when the larger integer is divided by the smaller integer is $4$. Find the two integers.
38.
A car covers a certain distance at a uniform speed of $60 \text{ km/h}$. If it travels at $50 \text{ km/h}$, it is late by $1 \text{ hour}$. Find the total distance.
39.
In a fraction, twice the numerator is $2$ more than the denominator. If $3$ is added to both the numerator and the denominator, the new fraction becomes $\frac{2}{3}$. Find the original fraction.
40.
Solve for $x$: $\frac{2x - 3}{4} + \frac{x + 1}{6} - \frac{5x - 2}{3} = \frac{1}{12}$
