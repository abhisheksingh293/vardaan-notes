SECTION E (12 Marks)(Case-Based Questions - 4 Marks Each)
36.
Case Study 1: Supermarket Sale
Rahul goes to an electronics supermarket to buy a new Smart TV. The marked price of the TV is ₹ 40,000. To attract customers during the festival season, the supermarket offers two successive discounts of 15% and 10% on the marked price.
(i) Calculate the price of the TV after the first discount of 15%. (1 Mark)
(ii) Calculate the final selling price that Rahul pays after the second discount. (2 Marks)
(iii) If the shopkeeper had offered a single flat discount of 25% instead of successive discounts, would Rahul pay more or less? By how much? (1 Mark)
37.
Case Study 2: Designing a Rectangular Park
A community is designing a new rectangular park in their locality. The dimensions of the park are given in terms of a variable \( x \) to allow for flexible planning. The length of the park is \( (4x + 3) \) meters and its breadth is \( (3x - 2) \) meters.
(i) Write the polynomial expression representing the total area of the park. (2 Marks)
(ii) Write the polynomial expression representing the perimeter of the park. (1 Mark)
(iii) If the final plan fixes the value of \( x \) as 10, find the numerical value of the perimeter in meters. (1 Mark)
38.
Case Study 3: Savings and Coins
Reema has a piggy bank containing a total of ₹ 300 in coins of denominations ₹ 1, ₹ 2, and ₹ 5. She counts her coins and realizes that the number of ₹ 2 coins is exactly 3 times the number of ₹ 5 coins. The total number of coins in the piggy bank is 160. Let the number of ₹ 5 coins be denoted by \( x \).
(i) Express the number of ₹ 2 coins and ₹ 1 coins in terms of \( x \). (1 Mark)
(ii) Form the linear equation that represents the total monetary value of the coins. (1 Mark)
(iii) Solve the equation to find the exact number of ₹ 5 coins Reema has. (2 Marks)
